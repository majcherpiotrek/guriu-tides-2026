Notes

  • Predictions are for Jijoca de Jericoacoara (2°47′S 40°31′W); Guriú lies ~10 km west on the same open coast, so times differ by only a few minutes.
  • Heights are relative to the local chart datum (lowest astronomical tide); a "0.2 m" low means very little water.
  • Source: temperaturadomar.pt harmonic predictions, cross-checked against tabuademares.com. These are not the official DHN (Brazilian Navy) tables – verify against the Camocim table before relying on exact minutes.
  • Wind, swell and atmospheric pressure can shift real water levels by 10–30 cm and the timing by tens of minutes.
